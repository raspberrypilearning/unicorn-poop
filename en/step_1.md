## Add text to the rows

In the **HTML tab** add the first two rows inside the body tags. You can replace the text after the `<p>` with your own words.

<div class="c-project-code">

--- code ---
---
language: html
filename: index.html
line_numbers: true
line_number_start: 7
line_highlights: 9-15
---
<body>

  <section class="row row-one">
    <p>Mostly glitter</p>
  </section>

  <section class="row row-two">
    <p>A SERIOUS ART</p>
  </section>

</body>
--- /code ---

</div>

### Now run your code
Check that the text appears in separate rows.
